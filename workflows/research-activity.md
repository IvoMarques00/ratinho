# Research Activity

## Trigger

An open question needs investigating.

## Preconditions

The question is stated concretely enough to search existing content against.

## Steps

1. Check existing `knowledge/` and `domains/` for prior findings. — invokes
   [Research](../skills/research.md).
2. Investigate. — invokes [Research](../skills/research.md).
3. Store findings as atomic notes, linked into a domain. — invokes
   [Knowledge Management](../skills/knowledge-management.md).
4. Write up findings clearly. — invokes [Documentation](../skills/documentation.md).

## Skills Referenced

- [Research](../skills/research.md)
- [Knowledge Management](../skills/knowledge-management.md)
- [Documentation](../skills/documentation.md)

## Outputs

New or updated `knowledge/` notes, linked into `domains/`.

## Filing

`knowledge/`; the relevant `domains/<name>/README.md`.
