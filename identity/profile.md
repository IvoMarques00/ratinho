# Profile

Durable information about Ivo — preferences, principles, and working style
that hold across projects and over years, not day-to-day state. Day-to-day
state belongs in `memory/` or `tasks/`, not here.

## Preferences

_To be filled in over time as real preferences emerge and prove durable —
e.g. preferred tools, languages, review style. Add an entry here only once
a preference has shown up consistently, not on a single instance._

## Principles

_Durable working principles — e.g. how correctness, simplicity, and
maintainability get weighed against speed. Populate as they're identified
through actual decisions, and cross-link to the relevant ADR in
`governance/decisions/` where a principle originated from a specific
decision._

## Working Style

_How work actually gets done — planning depth before execution, preferred
feedback loops, tolerance for ambiguity, etc._

## Decision Patterns

_Recurring patterns in how decisions get made — e.g. bias toward
reversibility, preference for boring/proven technology, etc. Link to
`governance/decisions/` entries that exemplify a pattern once one exists._

## Communication Style

_How Ivo prefers information presented — level of detail, directness,
format preferences._

## Long-Term Objectives

_Durable, multi-year objectives. Should mirror the top-level items in
`strategy/vision.md` — this section is the identity-level "why," vision.md
is the strategy-level "what."_

## Note on transient information

Nothing in this file should need updating weekly. If you find yourself
wanting to log "what I'm focused on this week," that belongs in
`strategy/roadmap.md` (active priorities) or `tasks/`, not here.
